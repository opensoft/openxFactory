# ideation-dashboard Specification

This delta is the corpus EXIT of `ideation-dashboard` — the largest promoted
specification in this corpus at 289,266 bytes, 102 requirements and 472
scenarios, against which 30 archived changes and 5 active changes carry deltas.
Every one of its 102 requirements leaves the `openxFactory` corpus. Under the
founding ruling of 2026-09-04 (`opensoft/openxFactory` issue #656) the workbench
becomes TWO open-source repositories and a layer of domain descendants below
them, and `openxFactory` keeps the CORPUS and its GOVERNANCE while the code that
reads the corpus leaves entirely.

**THIS IS A THREE-WAY SPLIT, WHICH THIS CORPUS HAS NEVER SEEN.** The wallet
extraction (`archive/2026-08-28-split-openxwallet-repo`) established the only
precedent for a capability exit — two whole capabilities left with their
successor location recorded, its first `## REMOVED Requirements` blocks. Here ONE
capability splits three ways, so the successor location is recorded PER
REQUIREMENT rather than per capability. That per-requirement map is this delta,
and it is deliberately the change's largest artifact: it is what substitutes for
the byte-identity floor the extraction cannot meet (see `design.md` § D6). Instead
of proving the bytes did not change, it proves every requirement has exactly one
home.

**THE MAP IS A READING, NOT A RULING, AND RATIFICATION IS OVER IT.** Brett ruled
the three-layer TEST (DIRECTION Q5, 2026-09-04T15:48Z) and, in RULING C2
(17:47Z), which side of it engineering vocabulary falls on — *"a clinician using
`MedxDox` never sees the word 'requirement'"*. He did NOT rule the assignment
requirement by requirement; the staging topic says so in terms
(*"The per-module assignment is therefore NOT ruled module by module — it is
design work"*). Each row below therefore carries the reason it was read that way,
so a row can be contested individually at the ratification read without
reopening the split.

**TITLES ARE REPRODUCED CHARACTER-FOR-CHARACTER FROM THE PROMOTED SPEC**, because
`scripts/doc_health/promotion_fidelity.py` keys on (capability, normalized title)
and one character of drift leaves the promoting writer authoritative and this
removal invisible to the checker. They were generated from
`openspec/specs/ideation-dashboard/spec.md` at `origin/main` rather than
retyped.

## THE SIBLING COLLISION, DECLARED RATHER THAN PAPERED OVER

**FIVE ACTIVE CHANGES CARRY A LIVE `ideation-dashboard` DELTA**, and RULING Q6
(2026-09-04T17:49Z) freezes all five where they stand and RE-HOMES them:
`add-composed-view-authoring`, `add-doxchat-model-intake`,
`add-lens-document-selection`, `add-nightly-dashboard-refresh` and
`retire-doxbench-chat-turn-v1`. The collision has two distinct shapes and they
are handled differently:

**(a) FOUR PROMOTED TITLES THIS BLOCK REMOVES ARE ALSO MODIFIED BY A SIBLING.**

| promoted requirement | sibling holding a `## MODIFIED` block | its destination here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Composed views are read-only with a repository jump | `add-composed-view-authoring` | openDox |
| doxBench model catalog and provider boundary | `add-doxchat-model-intake` | openDox |
| Runtime snapshot fetch with baked fallback and displayed freshness | `add-nightly-dashboard-refresh` | openXdox |
| The chat-turn contract release carries the bound buffer and the model | `retire-doxbench-chat-turn-v1` | openDox |

`release-realization`'s *Ordered deltas and branch vocabulary* governs a proposal
MODIFYING a requirement another active ratified change modifies or adds. **This
block REMOVES, so that requirement's antecedent is not met and this is an
EXTENSION of its reasoning to a shape no promoted requirement reaches, not an
application of it** — stated the way `add-binding-consumer-identity` was made to
state its own extension (LA-A3) rather than quoting an antecedent it does not
satisfy. `govern-sibling-added-modified-deltas`' reserved `Modified over` marker
is likewise a MODIFIED-BLOCK form and does not reach a REMOVED block; the pairing
is therefore declared in prose here, exactly as
`admit-deliberation-clearing-operation` declared its ADDED-block pairing in prose
for the same reason.

**THE ARCHIVE ORDER IS PART OF THE OBLIGATION, AND IT RUNS THE OPPOSITE WAY FROM
THE PROMOTED RULE.** Where a sibling ADDS and this change would MODIFY, the basis
archives first. Here this change REMOVES what a sibling MODIFIES, so **this
change SHALL NOT archive until each of the five is dispositioned** — archived, or
closed as re-homed with its successor destination named in the receiving
repository (`tasks.md` § 6). In the other order a sibling's MODIFIED block
promotes a requirement into a capability the corpus no longer carries, which
`document-lifecycle`'s *Ratified spec deltas reach the promoted specification*
would then report forever as a requirement present after its ratified removal.

**(b) TWELVE TITLES THE SIBLINGS ADD ARE NOT IN CANON AND ARE NOT REMOVED HERE.**
Four from `add-doxchat-model-intake`, five from `add-lens-document-selection`, one
from `add-nightly-dashboard-refresh`, two from `retire-doxbench-chat-turn-v1`. A
`## REMOVED Requirements` block over a title the promoted specification does not
carry would be a removal of nothing, and `openspec archive` would apply it
against a document that never held the title. They are handled by the re-homing
plan instead: under RULING Q6 those four changes stop where they stand, so their
`## ADDED` blocks never promote HERE and their content is re-authored in the
receiving repository. `tasks.md` § 6 names the destination for each of the twelve.

**`add-nightly-dashboard-refresh` ALSO ADDS SEVEN REQUIREMENTS TO `doc-health`,
AND THIS CHANGE AUTHORS NO `doc-health` DELTA.** RULING Q6 resolves that tension
by re-homing the change rather than re-authoring its seven requirements against
the adapter in place. `doc-health` stays `openxFactory`'s own capability and is
not split by this change; touching it here would also collide with
`settle-aging-staging-topics`, which holds the corpus's other active `doc-health`
delta.

## `ideation-intent-plane` IS NOT REMOVED HERE, AND THAT IS A GATE

`add-ideation-intent-plane` has been RATIFIED and part-realized since 2026-07-23
and its target capability `ideation-intent-plane` is **absent from
`openspec/specs/`** — ratified and unpromoted for 43 days. RULING Q1 has just made
its apply lane the ONLY governed write path. A capability that is not in canon
cannot be REMOVED from canon, so this delta says nothing about it and no delta on
it is authored here (its only live delta is held by that active change, and a
second writer would be a collision this change does not need). `tasks.md` § 0.6
makes its promotion — or a RECORDED non-promotion — a gate on the successor map's
realization rather than a follow-up.

## What a removal does NOT do

No `superseded` header is set on the promoted spec, no emptied stub is left as a
signpost, and no archived record is rewritten: the 30 archived packets carrying
an `ideation-dashboard` delta are ANNOTATED with the carry-forward, never edited
into agreement — the treatment the wallet arc used on
`docs/archive-record-discrepancies.md` and its own archived predecessor.
