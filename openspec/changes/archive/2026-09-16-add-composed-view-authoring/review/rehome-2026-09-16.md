## NO DELTA IS PROMOTED, AND THAT IS THE PACKET'S INSTRUCTION

The one `## MODIFIED` block is **not** applied to
`openspec/specs/ideation-dashboard/spec.md`. The packet says so twice — once as
the general rule for the five, *"their `## ADDED` blocks never promote HERE and
their content is re-authored in the receiving repository"*, and once in the
migration voice of this requirement's own successor row, *"the requirement is
re-promoted in `opensoft/openDox`'s own OpenSpec instance"*.

This is why the relocation is a `git mv` rather than `openspec archive` or
`scripts/proposal-support.py … archive`. **Both of those always promote**: the
pinned CLI's archive takes `--skip-specs`, and the governed wrapper does not
expose it (`proposal-support.py … archive --help` lists `--date`,
`--final-import-complete`, `--yes` and `--path-mode`, and nothing else). A
wrapper run followed by a hand-revert of the promotion hunk would have produced
the same tree through an act whose record said the opposite, so the act is the
one the record can state without qualification.

Nothing else the wrapper guards is lost by not running it: this packet carries
NO `.openspec.yaml`, so there is no `origin:` declaration to freeze and the
ORIGIN RETENTION gate has nothing to compare; and the archive directory name is
the one the wrapper would have computed — `<today in UTC>-<id>`, the id carrying
no date prefix of its own.

## THE COST OF NOT PROMOTING, STATED HERE RATHER THAN DISCOVERED BY THE NIGHTLY

`document-lifecycle`'s *Ratified spec deltas reach the promoted specification*
(`openspec/specs/document-lifecycle/spec.md:1055`) attaches the promotion
obligation to the ARCHIVE ACT, and presumes ratification from that act unless the
packet's own `proposal.md` declares `draft` or lower. This packet declares
`Status: ratified` and that header is NOT edited — a closure is not a licence to
restate what Brett ratified on 2026-08-08.

So the obligation attaches, and `scripts/doc_health/promotion_fidelity.py`
carries it at `_LAUNCH_SEVERITY = ERROR`. The checker's own action text names
both discharges:

> apply the ratified delta to the promoted spec through an OpenSpec change, **or
> record the non-promotion as deliberate**

This closure takes the second, and the recording vehicle is the one that checker
reads: `health/dispositions.yaml` at the **xFactory aggregation root**
(`load_dispositions()`, `promotion_fidelity.py:714-786`), an entry keyed
`family: promotion-fidelity` / `repo: openxFactory` / `path:` this packet's
archived delta, carrying a `cite` that names RULING Q6, this record and the
receiving pull request. That entry is a companion act in the aggregation
repository — it cannot live here — and it is batched across the five § 6
closures with `tasks.md` § 6.6 rather than filed five times.

**The caveat travels with it, because the loader states it in its own docstring:**
a `--single-repo` self-gate run has no aggregation root, so no disposition
applies in that scope. A self-gate run of this family will therefore report the
finding this record disposes, at every head, until the capability itself leaves
in § 5. Both numbers are reported on the closing pull request; neither is
suppressed.
