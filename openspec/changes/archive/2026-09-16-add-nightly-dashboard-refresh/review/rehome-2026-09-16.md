This is the one substantive judgement in the § 6 arc and it is recorded rather
than left in a diff.

§ 6.4's and § 6.3's deltas contained the string `openxFactory` **zero** times and
travelled untouched. These seven are openxFactory-nightly-specific in their
ANTECEDENTS, not in a subject noun — *"The nightly doc-health run SHALL include

and is unpromoted; the successor capability id is that packet's § 5.2a, unbuilt.

again the moment the seam is declared.

So the seven arrive at openXdox-spec byte-identical below their first line, on
openXdox's own `openxdox-refresh-lane` capability and **never on a `doc-health`
one** — `doc-health` stays `openxFactory`'s and Q4 points that dependency one way
— and the receiving change carries the re-authoring as a blocked open box
(`tasks.md` § 2.1) and BARS ratifying the seven until it lands (§ 2.2), because
openXdox would otherwise ratify obligations about another repository's nightly
run. **If a ruling directs that the re-authoring be performed now rather than

decision is visible.**
