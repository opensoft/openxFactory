## The lockstep pair this archive retires

`scripts/target-release-register.yaml` carried an `implementation_pending` entry
for this change whose `retires_when:` read: *"the declaration is corrected to the
target it realizes on, **or the packet archives**."* It archived — by the second
limb, and by a route the entry's author did not have in view: it was CLOSED AS
RE-HOMED rather than realized. The register effect is the one the text named: the
declaration leaves the ACTIVE corpus the register scans, and `Report.stale`
refuses until the entry is deleted.

**THE ENTRY'S DELETION IS REQUIRED; THE PAIR'S IS THIS PULL REQUEST'S CHOICE,
and an earlier draft of this record ran the two together.** `Report.stale`
REFUSES while the stale entry stands, so the entry must go. `CLOSED_REGISTER` is
under no such rule, and `scripts/target_release.py` says so in its own words:
*"A BASELINE MAY BE A STRICT SUPERSET, AND USUALLY WILL BE: removal is lawful (a
corrected declaration or an archived packet retires its entry, and
`Report.stale` REFUSES until the entry is deleted), so a pair stays here after
its entry goes. This is a ceiling, never a floor."* The module's other sentence —
an entry and its pair moving *"in the same pull request, where the diff shows the
act for what it is"* — governs ADMITTING a pair the baseline does not carry, the
addable direction the register refuses; it is not a removal requirement. Copilot
read that correctly on PR #1060 and the correction is taken here, in the
register's comment, and in the test comment, all three of which had it backwards.

So the pair moves as a DELIBERATE BASELINE SHRINK: lawful, in the same pull
request as the entry because that is where the movement is legible, and taken
rather than skipped because a ceiling left standing above a retired entry is a
line nothing ever reconciles again. The baseline falls from 21 pairs to 20,
`tests/target_release/test_target_release_gate.py`'s size literal follows it, and
each carries the reason beside it. Measured: `pytest -q tests/target_release` →
**96 passed**.

This is the same class of coupling § 6.4 found at `contracts/openspec-cli-pin.yaml`,
