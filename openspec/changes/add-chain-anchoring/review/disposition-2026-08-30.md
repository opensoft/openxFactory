## 3. THE RULED PROCESS ITEMS, STATED WHERE THEY BIND

### 3.1 Item 6 — THE ROUTING RULE

**A packet that NARROWS a constraint carried in a staged topic SHALL ROUTE the
narrowing to its council as an explicit decision, not merely RECORD it in its own
text.**

#513 did the hard half — the D8 narrowing is in its requirement text, in
`design.md`, and in the pull-request record — and then omitted it from the six
authoring decisions `tasks.md:2.2` put to the seats. **The convening had to add
it as ballot C-1.** The rule is #513's own sentence, now binding: *"a topic's
constraint is not a packet's to quietly reinterpret."*

**Recorded is not ruled.** This is a process obligation on future packets and
**not** a defect finding against #513, whose narrowing is ruled correct at item 6.

**A companion fact travels with it, because it is what made C-1 answerable:** a
constraint sitting inside a fully-ruled topic is **not thereby ruled**. The
anchor-late sentence lives in the topic's `## Conflicts` section — two of whose
three conflicts carry disposition stamps and this one does not — and none of the
seven Q-dispositions reaches it. **`## Conflicts` sections carry unruled material
by design.**

### 3.2 Item 7 — THE CSC GROUND IS RULED. THE DISSENT IS DISCHARGED.

**The honest ground for not seating the `client-security-compliance-officer` on a
§7.4 proposal review is *INAPPLICABLE*, not *FALSE*** — `lead-architect`'s
wording, adopted.

The predicate `rule_touches_security_posture` asks a **definition-time** question
about *"the rule being SET"*. **A §7.4 proposal review sets no rule**, so the
predicate has no subject. Returning `False` misreports an inapplicable predicate
as a negative answer; `ConditionUnevaluable` is what the code returns and, per
`seat_resolution.py:40-44`, obliges the caller to *"refuse the rule or park the
convening"* — with no rule here to refuse. **Naming the ground *inapplicable*
resolves both readings without weakening either.**

**`lead-security`'s LS-C4 park is DISPOSED by route (ii).** No supplementary CSC
seating is convened; **no client-layer exposure voice is required for a §7.4
proposal review that sets no rule.**

**THIS DISCHARGES A DISSENT THAT STOOD THROUGH THREE CONSECUTIVE SITTINGS** —
raised by `lead-security` on 2026-08-29, left undecided by that sitting's ruling
(record §8.5 expressly did not adopt a ground), re-raised and escalated to a park
here. **It is now ruled, and the seat that raised it got the answer it asked for
rather than a silence.**
