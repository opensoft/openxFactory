## Q8 — composition drift for hosted-model holders: FAIL-CLOSED BUNDLE

All four limbs ruled together, in the fail-closed direction:

1. **(a) Reissue is always an explicit register act.** No auto-reissue and no
   standing reissue policy that acts on its own: a composition change requires
   a new, human-ratified register act before authority continues.
2. **(b) DRIFT cascades like CAUSE.** A parent grant revoked for DRIFT
   (unattested composition change) revokes derived grants exactly as a CAUSE
   revocation does. Fail-closed: derived authority never survives an
   unattested drift. Mechanical cascade enforcement rides the
   `openxwallet` / `openxwallet-agent-profile` core deltas (S5-era work).
3. **(c) Empty-register notification rides the Human Escalation Contract's**
   parked decision-ready packet shape (N12): when the register empties or a
   holder's last active row expires, the operator receives the HEC's
   decision-ready packet — not a bare log line.
4. **(d) Exact model versions only.** A hosted holder may NOT pin a model
   family ("gpt-5.x"); today's validation failure is ratified as intended
   behavior. Family pinning may be revisited only through a future core delta
   that can police it.

## Q9 — the floor's source of truth: EXIT A (YAML CARRIER)

The single source of truth for codexFactory's never-clearable floor becomes a
seedable, schema-validated `.yaml` carrier; the `roles-authority-model`
record is demoted to evidence. Rationale: the floor is machine-consumed by
the decision core from base-branch YAML, schema validation gives fail-closed
guarantees at load, and a seedable carrier matches how the register itself
works on the neutral rail.

Follow-up: author the declared delta that (i) introduces/validates the
carrier schema, (ii) moves floor authority to it, (iii) demotes the record
text to evidence, and (iv) amends `add-substantive-review-lane`'s ratified
text accordingly. Until that delta lands, both artifacts continue to say the
same thing and the decision core keeps reading the gate config.

## Q10 — non-human authorizers: ADMIT WITH CONDITIONS

A non-human authorizer (an agent body such as MRC) MAY satisfy
`isolated_per_use_authorized`, but only against admission conditions that are
designed, recorded, and validated first. The §1.2 `act_unsupervised`
exclusion remains in force until those conditions exist. This ruling opens
the door deliberately; it does not widen authority today.

Follow-up: design the per-use admission conditions (candidate shape: per-use
attested sandbox/isolation evidence consumed by the validator), record them,
and wire their validation. Task 8.3 stays open until the conditions are
recorded — the direction alone does not admit anyone.
