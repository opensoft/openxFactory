A DRAFT active change. `dh:30-36` makes the family read it exactly as it reads a
ratified one: a draft block is as capable of restating stale canon as a ratified
one, the arms are advisory, and a finding against a draft costs its author one
line. Reading scope and normative obligation are different things.
