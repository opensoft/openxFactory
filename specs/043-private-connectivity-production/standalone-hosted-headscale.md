# Standalone Hosted Headscale production acceptance

Status: draft
Created: 2026-11-13
Feature: [043-private-connectivity-production](spec.md)

This track qualifies the independently offered managed network service on openXsoft.com. It also supplies the qualified service dependency for managed attachments. No subscription plan, price or provider is chosen by this specification.

### H-01: Use the actual intended platform and integrations

Qualify the real approved provider, identity, entitlement and credential-custody integrations. Two approved managed customer boundaries have separate instance, endpoint, keys, durable state and policy. Production and non-production are separate by default. Reject the Eagle synthetic provisioning adapter or fixture identities as production-provider evidence.

### H-02: Operate without a factory subscription

A currently entitled standalone customer can provision and operate the network without a factory subscription. Qualification proves legitimate enrollment, private use, administration, revocation and independently authorized service termination. Factory-subscription absence is not a denial reason for standalone network service.

### H-03: Prove customer and environment isolation

Test enrollment, administration, route and traffic attempts across customer and environment boundaries. Every denied attempt has a successful legitimate control from the correct boundary and actual supported clients on intended direct/relay paths. A broken service or absent probe does not establish isolation. Routes remain separately approved and network access grants no application permission.

### H-04: Detach a factory while retaining independent service

Cancel or detach one factory and interrupt/retry cleanup. The same originally approved attachment is removed within its approved revocation/cleanup bound. Independently entitled networks, unrelated authorized devices and neighboring customer availability survive. Network termination requires its own current scoped customer authority.

### H-05: Reconcile interrupted operations without widening authority

Interrupt provisioning, enrollment and cancellation before and after partial effects, including a service restart before retry. The original subjects and operation progress survive the restart. Retries retain stable operation identity and the immutable original subjects, rechecking current authority before effects and activation. Changed input, revoked entitlement, expired/replayed credentials and unsupported scope fail without duplicate resources, widened access or resurrection of canceled subjects. Evidence carries references and redacted outcomes, never raw secrets.

### H-06: Recover, contain and resume against measured objectives

Qualify upgrades, protected backup, fenced restore, current-revocation reconciliation, endpoint cutover and rollback under approved fault scopes. Measure availability/error, capacity, spend, revocation, backup age/data loss, restore, rollback, sampling and observation coverage. Exceeded limits or missing coverage fail acceptance. Containment preserves authorized neighbors; resume requires reviewed remediation, affected/neighbor reruns and fresh scoped authority.

## Qualification evidence

The hosted-product owner supplies supported service/integration and candidate evidence. The install owner supplies package/state evidence; the managing factory supplies governed execution, custody, monitoring and operational acceptance. Q2 requires all four offering paths across the program, not only this standalone service. Q3–Q5 distinguish a bounded service canary, canary acceptance and its commercial offer or expansion. See [owner scopes](owner-scopes.md) and [rollout gates](rollout-gates.md).
