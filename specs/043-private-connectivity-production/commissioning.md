Accepted, failed, missing, expired and superseded decisions remain distinguishable. Later values may remain prerequisites only until their consuming gate; an absent value never counts as an entry pass.

## Q0 acceptance rule

Q0 requires the ratified policy, a single coordinating feature, accepted C-01 scopes and the C-02 platform/target/domain decisions needed for the proposed implementation. It identifies the release/support scope, custody, operating objectives and cost/capacity constraints, including which fully populated later decisions must precede Q1/Q3.

A scope may be commissioned only to the extent all decisions it consumes are accepted. Provider-specific implementation cannot start while its platform is unselected. Numeric staging limits and supported candidates must be concrete before Q1; actual production limits and customer/fault bounds must be concrete before Q3. Naming a future decision does not approve it.

Q0 accepts only specifically commissioned implementation/preparation. Managed or customer-operated execution still requires C-03 at the relevant target gate. New governed owner surfaces first follow their owning OpenSpec process.
