`TAG OWED` from a notice to a warning once the declaring commit is some
first-parent landings back — the tag is meant to follow the declaration
promptly, and a branch that keeps committing after declaring a bundle is exactly
the drift it watches for. **CI does not evaluate this branch tip**: it evaluates
the PULL REQUEST'S MERGE TREE, where the declaration and the merge are one
landing apart, and the panel reproduced that as GREEN on the built merge tree.

tag is realization-order step 5, box 5.6, an [OPERATOR] act at the LANDED MERGE
COMMIT. Nothing here is repaired by editing the manifest, the changelog or the
inventory, and the gate's own action line says so.
