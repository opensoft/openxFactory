THE DECLARING-DISTANCE QUESTION, ASKED THE WAY CI ASKS IT. 2026-09-09.

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. openxFactory #866's cut commit records that bookkeeping
must ride the cut "because release-tag-gate grades the DECLARING DISTANCE: the
family emits no finding only when the earliest first-parent commit declaring the
bundle is the TIP, and a bookkeeping commit stacked on top of the declaration
puts it at distance one and refuses the gate. Measured on this branch before the
squash: '1 first-parent landing(s) after the commit that declared it', REFUSED
gate-findings." This lane's follow-up EVIDENCE-ONLY commit is exactly such a
stacked commit, so the question was MEASURED here rather than assumed either way.

THE RULE, from scripts/doc_health/release_tag_publication.py:
  * DEFAULT_THRESHOLD = 5 ("RULED BY BRETT HEAP, 2026-08-31: five first-parent
    landings").
  * distance == 0 -> no finding; 1..5 -> WARNING; > 5 -> ERROR; and
    `gate-findings` REFUSES on an error OR a warning. So a clean gate needs
    distance ZERO.
  * distance_from_tip() walks the FIRST-PARENT chain from `--head` and stops at
    the first commit whose manifest declares something else. It is the chain
    below the HEAD IT IS GIVEN that decides the answer.

WHAT CI GIVES IT. .github/workflows/release-tag-gate.yml runs `on: pull_request`
with actions/checkout@v4 and fetch-depth: 0, and its own header says the gate
"diffs the merge commit against its FIRST PARENT (the base tip)". That checkout
is `refs/pull/N/merge`, whose FIRST parent is the base tip and whose SECOND is
the branch tip. So the first-parent chain CI walks is [merge ref, main tip, ...]
— the merge ref declares contract-v3.6 and main declares contract-v3.5, the walk
breaks at index 1, and the distance is ZERO no matter how many commits the
BRANCH stacked above its own declaration.

CONCLUSION, STATED PLAINLY. Both readings are true of what they measure and they
do not contradict each other. The branch-tip reading is a WARNING at distance 1
because the branch's own first-parent chain carries the cut AND the evidence
commit, both declaring contract-v3.6. The merge-ref reading — the one the
required status check actually performs — is rc=0, "the release-tag obligation
holds over the merge tree 0feaa1ef4: no error, no warning".

SO THE FOLLOW-UP EVIDENCE-ONLY COMMIT DOES NOT RED THE GATE, and #866's rule is
narrower than its wording: what must not be stacked above the declaration is a
commit that would land on MAIN above it, which is what the family grades once the
cut is on main. It is still true, and still the safer discipline, that everything
the candidate CAN carry should ride the candidate — which is why every gate that
does not address the candidate by sha rides it here, and only the three that do
(--head, --commit, and the full suite) plus their transcripts follow.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY. It does not say the tag obligation is met. TAG OWED is
the notice at both readings, it is the expected state at a cut, and box 5.6
leaves it to the operator at the LANDED merge commit.
