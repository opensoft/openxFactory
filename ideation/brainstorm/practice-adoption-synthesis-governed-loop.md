## Possible feats

- **Practice adoption coordinator** — route a structured suggestion through
  client clearance, project realization, outcome observation, and catalog
  feedback without granting it merge authority.

## Members and their joints

Atomic members:
[Practice Suggestion Envelope](practice-adoption-suggestion-envelope.md)
and [Practice Clearance and Realization Ledger](practice-adoption-clearance-ledger.md).

### Suggestion creates a bounded proposal

Domain Hermes or another authorized observer emits evidence and a recommended
practice. The envelope identifies the affected tenant and projects but does
not broaden its read or write scope.

### Client clearance applies company policy

The client layer may auto-clear only inside a previously human-ratified
envelope. Unknown, consequential, or repeatedly exceptional conditions park
for the responsible authority.

### Projects realize through the normal front door

Each project chooses and implements the accepted change using its ordinary
proposal, branch, review, and acceptance workflow. The ledger pins that
evidence and later outcomes.

## Emergent behavior

The factory can learn from recurring gaps and improve itself without making
the observer, policy checker, implementer, and approver the same actor.

## Tensions to hold

- Faster auto-clearance increases throughput but can normalize weak defaults.
- Cross-project evidence improves learning while project-private details must
  stay scoped.
- Cost savings need counterfactual calibration rather than optimistic totals.

## Recombination opportunities

The loop consumes seeded authority from the
[Hermes runtime](hermes-overview.md) and may feed stable recurrence into the
[crystallization packet](crystallization-overview.md).
