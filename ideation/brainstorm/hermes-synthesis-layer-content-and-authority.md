### Content is composed before runtime use

Domain, client, and project content form a stricter layered overlay. The
seeding mechanism resolves pins, verifies digests, validates composition, and
splits content into enforceable records, memory bindings, and persona
references.

### Character is bounded by authority

Persona traits and prose shape attention, voice, and reasoning style. Owned
decisions, policy, consent, and escalation rules determine whether an action
is allowed.

### Legal constraints fail closed

Legal and compliance concerns are stored and routed as governed constraints.
Novel legal interpretation escalates to a human rather than becoming a model
decision.

## Emergent behavior

Hermes can present distinct, consistent deciders whose actual authority is
derived from validated layer content rather than prompt personality.

## Tensions to hold

- Rich character improves human comprehension but can obscure the real rule
  source.
- Deterministic seeding needs escape paths for emergency policy correction.
- A safe structural-only fallback may be operationally limited.

## Recombination opportunities

The seeded layers provide authority inputs to the
[governed practice loop](hermes-synthesis-governed-practice-loop.md).

## Open questions
