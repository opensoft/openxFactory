### Same neutral closure motion

Both domains need:

```text
seed partial evidence
  -> discover relationships and gaps
  -> acquire authorized sources
  -> process and reconcile
  -> assemble purpose views
  -> measure coverage
  -> declare readiness and open obligations
  -> resume when evidence or conditions change
```

The durable episode and recursive frontier are therefore plausible neutral
mechanisms. Neither domain can honestly use document count or vector-index size
as the readiness standard.

### Different domain denominators

Ledgerx coverage may be organized by legal entity, fiscal period, account
family, counterparty materiality, agreement set, and accounting workflow.
Medx coverage may be organized by care period, provider, encounter, medication,
lab, imaging, pathology, treatment exposure, and active clinical question.

The neutral contract can standardize coverage dimensions and state semantics
while each domain owns required facets and materiality.

### Different authority and harm profiles

Ledgerx extraction cannot update a ledger, vendor card, payment instruction,
tax treatment, or legal position. Medx inference cannot create a diagnosis,
chart correction, order, treatment change, or patient-facing clinical
conclusion.

Both require professional review for selected interpretations, but the
reviewer roles, urgency, exceptions, and consequences are domain content.

### Closure is qualified, not absolute

An organization can be established for AP intake while payroll or tax evidence
remains excluded. A patient can be ready for appointment coordination while
prior pathology remains a blocker for a treatment decision. One neutral
coverage ledger can support several workflow-specific readiness views.

## Emergent behavior

The two proof profiles show that recursive subject establishment is broader
than document ingestion and narrower than autonomous domain action. It is a
neutral evidence-and-model closure capability whose useful output is a
qualified, reviewable subject state plus an explicit map of what remains
unknown.

## Tensions to hold

- Cross-domain standardization can improve tooling while accidentally
  importing clinical or accounting meanings into the neutral kernel.
- A lenient `established_with_gaps` state enables progress but can become a
  permanent excuse for missing material evidence.
- High-assurance Medx requirements should stress the kernel without forcing
  every artifact-subject domain to bear person-subject controls it does not
  need.

## Recombination opportunities

The profiles can become additional evidence for the staged neutral
subject-establishment capability, while remaining brainstorm material until a
proposal deliberately selects the recursive evidence-establishment delta.
Later codexFactory and OpsxFactory profiles could test engineering-project and
managed-estate evidence closure without changing these domain examples.

## Open questions

- Which readiness states and coverage dimensions are truly neutral?
- Does Medx require a separate high-assurance conformance tier from the first
